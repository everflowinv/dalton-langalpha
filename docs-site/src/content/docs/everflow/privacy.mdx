本页说明 Everflow 部署的 Dalton（以下称「Dalton」）在你连接第三方账号后，如何使用、保存和删除相关数据。English version below.

## 适用范围

本政策适用于你在 Dalton 网页「设置 → 个人连接」中主动连接的账号：

- **Google 日历**：查看日程，在你确认后创建、修改或删除日程与提醒。
- **Gmail**：在对话中按你的要求查找、阅读邮件，并在你确认后保存草稿。邮件由你本人在 Gmail 中发出。
- **飞书日历与飞书邮箱**：查看日程与邮件，在你确认后创建或修改日程；开启「允许在对话中发送邮件」后，经你逐封确认发送邮件。

## 我们如何使用这些数据

- 只为完成你在对话或你设置的定时任务中提出的请求而读取数据，例如回答「我下周有哪些会」、整理指定邮件、按你的财报日历安排提醒。
- 使用的是你本人的授权。研究总监等助手代你操作时，同样只能使用你本人的连接。
- 创建、修改、删除日程和发送邮件前，都会先给你看确认卡片，由你本人确认。
- 来自 Google 用户数据的内容只用于向你提供上述功能，用于训练任何通用人工智能或机器学习模型的情形一律排除，出售或转让给第三方的情形也一律排除。Dalton 对 Google API 所获数据的使用遵守 [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy)，包括其中的 Limited Use（有限使用）要求。

## 谁能看到

- 个人日历与邮件内容只出现在你本人的私聊或网页私人会话中。
- 在群聊或团队共享的工作区中提出相关请求时，Dalton 会引导你到私聊中查看。
- 包含个人日历或邮件内容的对话，在公开分享时按分享规则隐去相关内容。

## 保存多久

- **授权凭据**（访问令牌、刷新令牌）加密保存在 Dalton 的数据库中，在你断开连接时删除。
- **对话记录**中由你请求产生的回答，与你的其他对话一样保存在你的账号下，你可以随时删除对话。
- **派生的技术信息**（例如为检索建立的向量索引）最多保存 30 天。
- 断开连接或在 Google / 飞书一侧撤销授权后，Dalton 会删除通过该连接取得、仍在保存中的数据。

## 断开连接与删除数据

- 在 Dalton「设置 → 个人连接」中点击「断开」即可停止访问并删除授权凭据。
- 也可以在 [Google 账号的第三方访问页面](https://myaccount.google.com/connections) 或飞书的授权管理中撤销授权，Dalton 会同步处理。
- 需要删除其他相关数据时，请联系你的团队管理员，或通过 [Everflow 官网](https://everflowinv.com) 上的联系方式与我们联系。

## 变更

本政策更新时，会修改本页顶部的更新日期。

## English version

This page explains how Dalton, as deployed by Everflow ("Dalton"), uses, stores and deletes data from third-party accounts that you choose to connect.

### Scope

This policy covers accounts you connect yourself under **Settings → Personal connections**:

- **Google Calendar**: view your events and, after you confirm, create, update or delete events and reminders.
- **Gmail**: search and read messages when you ask in a conversation, and save drafts after you confirm. You send the draft yourself from Gmail.
- **Feishu Calendar and Feishu Mail**: view events and mail; create or update events after you confirm; send mail only when you turn on sending and confirm each message.

### How we use the data

- Data is read only to fulfil requests you make in a conversation or in scheduled tasks you set up.
- Dalton always acts with your own authorization, including when an assistant acts on your behalf.
- You confirm every create, update, delete or send action on a confirmation card first.
- Dalton's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide these features to you. It is never used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models, and it is never sold or transferred to third parties.

### Who can see it

- Personal calendar and mail content appears only in your own direct messages or private web conversations.
- Requests made in group chats or shared team workspaces are redirected to a private conversation.
- Public share links hide personal calendar and mail content.

### Retention

- Access and refresh tokens are stored encrypted and deleted when you disconnect.
- Answers produced at your request stay in your conversation history, which you can delete at any time.
- Derived technical data, such as search indexes, is kept for at most 30 days.
- When you disconnect, or revoke access on the Google or Feishu side, Dalton deletes the data it obtained through that connection that it still holds.

### Disconnecting and deletion

- Click **Disconnect** under Settings → Personal connections to stop access and delete stored tokens.
- You can also revoke access from your [Google Account connections page](https://myaccount.google.com/connections) or Feishu's authorization settings.
- For other deletion requests, contact your team administrator or reach us through the contact details on the [Everflow website](https://everflowinv.com).

### Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated.
